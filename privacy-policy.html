Collector Confidentiality & Data Protocol

AARTIGO Atelier respects the utmost discretion and privacy required by our private collectors, architects, and institutions.

1. Private Collection Discretion

All collector identities, architectural floorplans, residential addresses, and private commission specifications are held under strict confidentiality agreements. We never disclose provenance or spatial placements without written authorization.

2. Curatorial Enquiries & Consultations

Information submitted through our spatial consultation and commission Enquiry forms is used solely to facilitate direct founder-led communication regarding bespoke sculptural projects.

3. Atelier Correspondence

Collectors subscribed to our private communications receive invitations to newly completed solitary artworks, monograph releases, and private exhibitions. You may opt out at any time.
