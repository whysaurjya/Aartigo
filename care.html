Care & Handle

Every Aartigo piece is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, combining artistry, material integrity, and enduring craftsmanship. Proper care ensures that your artwork retains its beauty, character, and finish for years to come.

01 · Interior Collections

Indoor Pieces

Indoor artworks and furnishings require gentle, routine care to maintain their finish and appearance.

Recommended Care

  • Dust regularly using a soft, dry cloth.
  • Vacuum uneven, textured, or chiselled surfaces gently when necessary.
  • Use a slightly damp cloth only for stubborn dirt or marks.
  • Keep the piece away from prolonged direct sunlight to help prevent discoloration and fading.

Avoid

  • Chemical cleaners, detergents, or abrasive cleaning products.
  • Any cleaning solution that may affect or remove the protective top coat.
  • Excessive moisture or soaking.
Important Precaution Before using any cleaning product, always test it on a small, hidden area first.
02 · Landscape & Architectural

Outdoor Pieces

Outdoor pieces are designed to withstand environmental conditions, but regular maintenance will help preserve their finish and longevity.

Recommended Care

  • Clean with mild soap and water.
  • Remove dust and dirt gently using a soft cloth or brush.
  • Dry the piece after cleaning to avoid water spots.
  • Clean periodically to maintain the appearance of the finish.
  • Cover the piece when not in use for extended periods.

Avoid

  • Harsh chemicals, strong detergents, and abrasive cleaners.
  • Power washing or high-pressure cleaning, as it may damage the protective coating.
03 · Secure Transit & Placement

Transportation & Unpacking

Each Aartigo piece is carefully packed to ensure safe transit. Please handle all packages with care during unpacking and installation.

Unpacking Guidelines

  • Unpack slowly and carefully, as artworks and handcrafted pieces can be fragile.
  • Keep the box upright throughout the unpacking process.
  • Store packaging materials away from moisture.
  • Inspect the artwork and any electrical components (where applicable) before use.
  • Never drop, drag, or subject the package to rough handling.

Assembly & Installation

  • Follow all assembly and installation instructions provided with the piece.
  • Ensure installation is completed carefully and on suitable surfaces.
  • If you require assistance, our team will be happy to provide guidance and support.
